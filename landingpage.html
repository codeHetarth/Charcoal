About Charcoal

Charcoal is a lightweight note-taking app built for people who think in markdown. Write in the browser, keep your notes organized, and export clean .md files whenever you need them.

What you can do?

  • Write without friction — open the editor and start typing. Titles and content save automatically as you work.
  • Manage your notes — create, switch between, and delete notes from a simple sidebar. No clutter, no learning curve.
  • Export to markdown — download any note as a .md file, ready for GitHub, documentation, or your favorite editor.
  • Work your way — try Charcoal instantly in guest mode, or sign in to keep your notes saved to your account.

Why Charcoal?

Most note apps try to do everything. Charcoal does one thing well: help you capture ideas in markdown and move them wherever you need to go. Whether you are drafting README files, jotting lecture notes, or keeping project docs in one place, Charcoal stays out of your way so you can focus on writing.
