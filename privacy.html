3. Information We Collect

When you use Charcoal, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password, stored in a securely hashed form
  • Notes and other content created by you
  • Information necessary to operate and maintain your account
  • Session information necessary to keep you securely logged in

If you use guest mode without creating an account, your notes are stored temporarily in a session on the server and are associated with your browser session. Guest notes are not saved to a user account and are removed when the session ends or the browser is closed.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and operate the application's functionality.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR, where applicable:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): to create and manage your account, authenticate your login, save and retrieve your notes, and provide account-related features such as password recovery and account deletion.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): to maintain, secure, and improve the application, prevent misuse, and protect users and the service.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): where you explicitly agree to specific processing, such as accepting these policies during registration. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

5. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Authenticate your login
  • Save and retrieve your notes
  • Provide account-related features such as password recovery
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the application
  • Protect the security of the application

We do not use your notes or personal information for advertising or personalized marketing.

6. Cookies and Session Information

Charcoal uses a session cookie to maintain your login session and allow the application to identify your authenticated session.

This cookie is essential for the operation and security of the application and is not used for advertising or tracking your activity across other websites.

  • Purpose: authentication, session management, and temporary storage of guest-mode notes
  • Type: HTTP cookie (httpOnly, sameSite: lax)
  • Duration: up to 7 days while you remain logged in; guest sessions end when you close your browser
  • Provider: Charcoal (first-party cookie)

Charcoal does not currently use cookies for advertising, personalised marketing, or non-essential tracking.

7. Storage of Information

User account information and notes for registered users are stored in a PostgreSQL database connected to the application's backend server.

Passwords are not stored as plain text. They are securely hashed before being stored.

Data is processed and stored on the server hosting the application. If the application is deployed on infrastructure located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), appropriate safeguards will be used where required by applicable data protection law.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information or intentionally share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

To provide certain account-related features, some information may be processed by third-party service providers that operate essential parts of the application.

Charcoal uses Resend as an email delivery service to send transactional emails, including account registration emails, password recovery emails, and account deletion confirmation emails. Information such as your email address and the content necessary to send these emails may be processed by Resend for this purpose. Resend may process data in the United States or other countries outside the EEA. Where required, such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards under applicable data protection law.

These services are used to operate the application and are not used by Charcoal for advertising or personalised marketing.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Passwords are securely hashed before being stored and are never stored as plain text.

However, no online system or method of electronic transmission can guarantee complete security.

10. Your Data Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request information about your stored data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request access to your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Receive your data in a portable format where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@yourwebsite.com. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Germany, you may contact the data protection authority (Landesdatenschutzbehörde) responsible for your place of residence.

11. Data Retention

Personal information and notes are retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or as required by applicable legal obligations.

When an account is deleted, associated account information and notes may also be deleted, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

Guest-mode notes are retained only for the duration of your browser session.
